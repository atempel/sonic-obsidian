01 — HOW IT WORKS

Play anything. Watch it come alive.

The visualizers we loved lived inside Winamp. Music moved to Spotify, YouTube and games, and they never followed. Sonic Obsidian sits next to all of them and turns whatever you play into light.

01

It hears what you play

Play something in Spotify, YouTube, a game or a DAW, and Windows loopback hands that playback to the visualizer. Nothing to route, no virtual cables.

48.0 kHz · STEREO → MONO MIX
02

It renders

Butterchurn, the WebGL port of MilkDrop 2, runs the warps, shaders and blends on your GPU, with presets made by two decades of MilkDrop artists.

WEBGL 2 · 60 FPS TARGET · 2.7 s BLENDS
03

It gets out of the way

No splash, no login. It opens straight into the visual; controls show up when you move the mouse and disappear when you stop.

06 — QUESTIONS

Fair questions.

Does it record or send my audio anywhere?

No. Audio is analyzed in memory to drive the visuals and is never saved or uploaded.

Is it safe if I'm sensitive to flashing lights?

Photosensitivity warning. Sonic Obsidian shows fast-flashing lights, strobing colors and moving patterns, by design. A small number of people may have seizures triggered by them, even with no history of epilepsy.

If you or anyone in your family has photosensitive epilepsy or has had seizures, talk to a doctor before using it. Stop and look away right away if you feel dizzy or nauseous, or notice blurred vision, eye or face twitching, or disorientation.

Watch in a well-lit room, keep some distance from the screen and take breaks. If your system is set to reduce motion, this page waits for you to press play before anything moves.

Is it really free?

Yes. MIT-licensed, no account, no ads, no paid tier. The code, the issues and the build pipeline are public on GitHub.

Why only Windows?

The whole point is capturing system audio with zero setup, and Windows exposes that cleanly through loopback. macOS and Linux may work by accident; they're not tested or supported.

Does it work with Spotify, YouTube, games?

Anything that plays through your speakers. Some DRM-protected streams may arrive silent; Sonic Obsidian doesn't try to get around that protection.

Can I make my own presets?

On the roadmap: importing .milk files from the community and authoring your own. Until then, the full Butterchurn library is in.

Can an AI drive it?

Planned: an MCP interface, so the AI tool you already use can create presets and steer the show live. Sonic Obsidian itself ships no AI and needs none.
